# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, no framework or build step. Hosted on Cloudflare Pages from the GitHub repo `hankmengedoht-hue/christmastoursite`.

## Users

Charleston visitors and locals deciding how to spend an evening the week before Christmas. Most arrive on a phone, spend 20–30 seconds, and either call for tickets or leave.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site introducing the Christmas in Charleston Candlelight Tour of Private Homes and getting visitors to call Laura Wichman Hip for tickets. Success is a phone call to 843-708-2228.

## Positioning

Homeowners open their own private downtown Charleston homes and lead the tours themselves, over two evenings, and the proceeds restore a historic church: New Tabernacle Fourth Baptist Church.

## Operating Context

Source of truth for tour facts is the Explore Charleston listing:
https://www.charlestoncvb.com/events/christmas-in-charleston-candlelight-tour-of-private-homes~28702/

Confirmed facts (from that listing and the owner's brief):

- Name: Christmas in Charleston Candlelight Tour of Private Homes
- Dates: Friday, December 18 and Saturday, December 19, 2026
- Hours: 4:00–8:30 (evening)
- Location: Downtown Charleston, SC 29401
- Friday: guests drive to the homes on their own; it is not a walking tour
- Saturday: the homes are within walking distance of each other
- Homeowners give the tours of their homes
- Benefits "the Restoration of an Architectural Gem for our Nation's 250th"; proceeds benefit the restoration of New Tabernacle Fourth Baptist Church
- Tickets: call 843-708-2228
- Contact: Laura Wichman Hip, 843-708-2228

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Tickets are sold by phone only; there is no online booking, form, or email.
- Undecided / not supplied: ticket price, which homes or how many, meeting or starting point, parking, accessibility, what a ticket includes, age guidance. These stay as visible-in-source placeholders and must not be invented.
- Visual reference named by the owner: christmasincharleston.com (quality and tone only; no copied text, branding, layout, or assets).

## Evidence on Hand

- No tour photography supplied. The owner asked for empty image placeholders until real photographs exist; do not ship stock photos.
- No logo, email address, social accounts, testimonials, or pricing. None may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- The phone number is the product's only conversion; it is never more than one glance away.
- Say only what is confirmed; mark the rest as pending.
- The two evenings differ (drive vs. walk) and visitors must be able to tell them apart at once.
- The cause (the church restoration) is part of the reason to come, not a footnote.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessible color contrast, keyboard navigation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support are required by the brief.
