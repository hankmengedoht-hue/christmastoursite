Discover Charleston

Charleston at Christmas, seen from the inside.

For two evenings in December, private homes in downtown Charleston open their doors. The homeowners themselves give the tours, from 4:00 to 8:30, in houses you would otherwise only pass on the street.

The tour benefits the restoration of an architectural gem for the nation’s 250th: New Tabernacle Fourth Baptist Church.

Learn More

What the evening holds

  1. Private homes

    Downtown Charleston houses, open to visitors for the evening.

  2. Shown by their owners

    The homeowners give the tours of their own homes.

  3. Two evenings

    On Friday you drive to the homes. On Saturday the homes are within walking distance of each other.

  4. A church restored

    Proceeds benefit the restoration of New Tabernacle Fourth Baptist Church.
