Discover Charleston

Charleston at Christmas, seen from the inside.

For two evenings in December, private homes in downtown Charleston open their doors. The homeowners themselves give the tours, from 4:00 to 8:30, in houses you would otherwise only pass on the street.

The tour benefits the restoration of an architectural gem for the nation’s 250th: New Tabernacle Fourth Baptist Church.
